Frequently asked questions

Good things to know before launch

Does FitBite use health data?

FitBite may process health-related information you enter or choose to connect, such as nutrition entries, goals, body measurements, and progress values.

Is FitBite medical advice?

No. FitBite is a tracking tool. It is not a medical service, diagnosis, treatment, or replacement for a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need an account?

Some features may work locally. Account-based features, sync, premium access, and support requests may require login.

How are payments handled?

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. FitBite does not receive your full payment card details.
